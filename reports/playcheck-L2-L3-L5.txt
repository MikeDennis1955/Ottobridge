How to read it
- DD par = tricks declarer takes with perfect play by everyone, seeing all cards. Scripted = what the script makes.
- SLIP  = a card by declarer's side that costs a trick double dummy. Often fine single dummy (e.g. a finesse
          that loses, or a safe line that gives up a DD-only overtrick). Worth a look when it is large or the note
          doesn't explain it.
- GIFT  = a defender's card that hands declarer a trick. Real defenders rarely do this: each one needs a look.
- LEAD  = a blind opening lead that costs a trick. Usually fine.
- Scripted above par means the defense gave something away somewhere.
